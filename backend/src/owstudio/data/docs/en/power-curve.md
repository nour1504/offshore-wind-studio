Speeds must increase, units must be correct and Ct plausible. Specific power is rated power divided by swept area. Cp and the Betz limit are consistency checks under stated assumptions; they do not replace manufacturer data validation.

Curve estimates use extracted turbine winds. They differ from WRF scheme power, which has its own subgrid calculations. Every result must identify its method.

Official reference : [WRF User Guide](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/) · [WPS](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/wps.html) · [WRF Physics](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/physics.html).
