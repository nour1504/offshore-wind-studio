Choose a run and domain. Maps and series use actual available outputs; times are UTC. Hub-height values are interpolated from levels using the stated method. Vector winds are rotated to geographic axes.

EP is energy over the simulated period. Capacity factors use covered duration; gaps are not zeros. AEP requires adequate annual coverage and is not long-term climate correction.

Grid-cell, farm and turbine outputs have different assumptions. Read method notes and export CSV series to retain units and metadata.
