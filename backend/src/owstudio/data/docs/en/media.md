Animations use actual wrfout times. Choose domain, variable, height, range, frame stride and video fps. Frames are rendered one at a time; video fps does not alter physical sampling.

Reports retain configuration, namelists and available results with methods. Without outputs, they are configuration reports. Review coverage and scientific caveats before sharing.

Exports have dedicated folders shown in Settings. Cancelling a render does not remove WRF data.

Official reference : [WRF User Guide](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/) · [WPS](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/wps.html) · [WRF Physics](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/physics.html).
