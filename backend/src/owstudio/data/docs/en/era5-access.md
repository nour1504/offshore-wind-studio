ERA5 downloads use your Copernicus CDS account. Accept dataset terms on the website and enter your key in Settings. No key is needed to import files already downloaded.

Downloads can be slow or queued/limited. Check period, area, pressure levels and necessary surface/soil fields. Do not include your key in exported projects.

Official reference : [WRF User Guide](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/) · [WPS](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/wps.html) · [WRF Physics](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/physics.html).
