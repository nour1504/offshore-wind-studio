Choose a variable: **Wind and turbulence** gathers the quantities computed from the outputs (hub-height wind, direction, turbulence intensity...), **WRF variables** those written as they are in the history files (wrfout). The bubble next to the name gives its definition. A field missing from the outputs is reported, never replaced.

The map shows the variable at the time chosen on the slider, at the given height for a variable on levels, in the chosen domain. Times are UTC.

A click on the map, or a tslist point in **Point**, gives the series of that point in **At the point**; **Export** opens it in the [Export](doc:data-export) tab. With a run without turbines imported, **Without turbines** shows the same variables for that control run.

Official reference: [WRF User Guide](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/) · [WPS](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/wps.html) · [WRF Physics](https://www2.mmm.ucar.edu/wrf/site/documentation/users_guide/physics.html).
